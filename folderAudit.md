Folder Audit Checklist
1. Patient Identification - care card/registers/EMR
o	Unique identifier 
o	Full name and contact information
o	Date of birth and gender
o	Details of next of kin (phone number) Ensure care card is completed (front, center and back page)
o	Pregnancy/breastfeeding status recorded (if applicable)
o	Transfer to recent version of care card (2021) 
2.	Medical History- folder/care card
o	Initial Adult/Pediatric clinical evaluation
o	Co-morbid conditions (HTN, DM, TB, Cryptococcal Meningitis)
3.	Clinical Assessments – care card/registers/EMR
o	Visit encounter date
o	Vital signs 
o	BMI
o	WHO staging
o	Functional status
o	TB evaluation
o	Baseline CD4 count for Tx New
o	Viral load order (tick) & VL result (value & date)
o	AHD diagnostic cascade 
4.	Treatment Information- care card/POF/register/EMR
o	Antiretroviral therapy (ART) regimen details (code according to the care card version) 
o	Days of ARV refill recorded
o	ART start date recorded and consistent
o	Adherence to ART, Cotrim & INH
o	Last drug pickup date available
o	Records of any changes in ART regimen and reasons
o	Next appointment date
5.	Laboratory Tests – care card/folder/EMR
o	Client intake form
o	HTS request and result form
o	Laboratory order form
o	Viral load (Last viral load test date, current viral load results)
o	VL result in care card & hard copy 
o	Previous VL result documented (get from EMR and document on the care card)
o	Evidence of Enhanced adherence counselling (form) for unsuppressed clients (check for completeness of sessions) 
o	Repeat VL test result post EAC session
6.	Medication Records- care card/POF/EMR/register/Pharmacovigilance form
o	Prescription details
o	Pharmacy refill records – Pharmacy Order Forms
o	Documentation of any side effects or adverse reactions
7.	Counselling and Support Services- care card/referral forms/violence prevention
o	Records of counselling sessions (e.g., adherence counselling, mental health support)
o	Index testing/Index Partner forms
o	Referrals to support services (e.g., nutritional support, social services, CCMP, FP)
o	Abstinence, safer sex, prevention with positives, support group etc.
o	IPV risk assessment and post violence response
8.	Preventive Measures- care card/TPT card/register/EMR
o	TB screening documented
o	Prophylaxis for opportunistic infections (e.g., cotrimoxazole)
o	INH dispensing
o	TPT start and completion date  
9.	Follow-Up and Monitoring- care card/registers/forms/EMR
o	Adherence to scheduled appointments
o	Evidence of missed appointment tracking and follow-up actions (CT&DF)
o	Follow up action taken for defaulters
10.	Pediatrics- care card, registers/EMR
o	Current regimens follow national guidelines and are age‑ and weight‑appropriate.
o	Clinical monitoring is reviewed to ensure growth, OI screening, and TB screening are routinely done at each clinic visit.
o	Viral load monitoring is evaluated, including timely testing (6-monthly for 0-9 years and 12-monthly for 10-19 years), documentation of results,
o	Completion of enhanced adherence counselling for unsuppressed clients.
o	Screening of adolescents for violence and provision of support services.

11.	PMTCT (patient identification, clinical assessment, treatment information as above) care card, registers/ EMR
o	Date of 1st ANC Visit and subsequent visits
o	Partner HIV status documented
o	Repeat HIV test (if negative initially)
o	Date of PMTCT enrolment /ART start date 
o	Pregnancy status and gestational age
o	Expected date of delivery (EDD)
o	Routine antenatal labs (PCV, HBV, Syphilis test)
o	Follow-up viral load at appropriate interval (baseline, 3 months after baseline, 32-36 weeks-newly identified; baseline, 32-26 weeks- Previously Known
o	Pregnancy complications recorded
o	Place of delivery documented
o	Date and mode of delivery 
o	Infant ARV prophylaxis given and date
o	Infant feeding option documented
o	Exclusive breastfeeding
o	Replacement feeding
o	Infant Cotrimoxazole start date
o	DNA PCR sample collected /NAAT and date (Birth, 6weeks, 6-12 weeks post cessation of breast feeding)
o	PCR results documented
o	Repeat PCR where applicable
o	Final HIV status documented
